# Crave It, I Create It  
## Mobile Kitchen App — Product Overview

**Live site:** https://crave-it-create-it.vercel.app  

Built for your trucks. Owned and maintained so you can run the business from a phone.

---

### What this is

A custom phone-installable app for your Las Vegas mobile kitchens. Customers find the truck, see what’s left, and place an order. Your drivers and kitchen run stock and orders from the same install.

Not a generic website template. Built around **your** model: each truck is its own kitchen with its own stock.

---

### What customers get

- **Live map** — see where the kitchens are right now  
- **Live stock** — what’s left on each truck before they order  
- **Order** — meet the truck or nearby drop-off  
- **Track** — look up the order with phone + order ID  
- **Concept Lab** — “you crave it, I create it” — submit custom tamal ideas  
- **Catering / events** — request trays, parties, job sites  
- **Install on phone** — Add to Home Screen (iPhone & Android). No App Store wait  

---

### What you and your team get

- **Driver board** — pick a truck, ping GPS so customers see the real spot, advance orders, watch stock  
- **Kitchen / Ops board** — start/stop routes, assign stock to trucks, mark sold out, see open orders and Concept Lab ideas  
- **Staff access** — locked behind a staff code so the public can’t run your boards  
- **Card payments (Stripe)** — customers pay at checkout; money goes to **your** Stripe account  
- **Your real menu & photos** — your food, your prices, your story  
- **Durable order & inventory data** — orders and stock that stick in real day-to-day use  

---

### How it works day to day

1. Customer opens the app (or the link).  
2. Sees trucks + what’s left.  
3. Adds items from **one** truck, checks out, **pays**.  
4. Order hits the board; stock comes off that truck.  
5. Driver / kitchen advances the order until it’s done.
